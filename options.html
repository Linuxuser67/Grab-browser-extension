Grab

When on, downloads are cancelled in the browser and opened in Grab instead. Blob and data URLs can't be handed off and always stay in the browser.

MB

Smaller downloads stay in the browser. 0 intercepts everything.

Comma-separated extensions, e.g. pdf, jpg.

Offered qualities appear in the right-click menu under “Videos detected by Grab”.

Pairing

With a code, the extension checks that it is talking to Grab, signs what it sends, and shares the site's cookies so Grab can download pages that need them. Without one, only links are sent. Leave empty to unpair.
